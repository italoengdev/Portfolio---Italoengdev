BusQme

A service marketplace that brings search, availability, booking, payments, and reviews into one experience.

Open live productBack to projects
RoleBubble.io developer
Product typeService marketplace
FocusBooking and transaction flows

Overview

BusQme was designed around the complete service-booking journey: finding a provider, understanding availability, completing a payment, and sharing feedback afterwards.

What I worked on

  • Built marketplace workflows and service discovery in Bubble.io.
  • Connected Stripe-based payment flows and booking logic.
  • Structured reviews and availability data for a clearer user experience.

Technology

Bubble.ioStripe APIDatabase designReal-time availability

Delivery

The product gives customers and providers a shared workflow for turning a search into a completed service interaction.
