Overview

The Adult Hockey League platform centralizes the operational work around a season: team payments, financial tracking, and dashboards that help organizers and participants follow what is happening.

What I worked on

  • Built responsive Bubble.io workflows for league administration.
  • Integrated Stripe for team payment flows and tracking.
