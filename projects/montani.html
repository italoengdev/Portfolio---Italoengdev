Case Study / Mobile Product

Montani

A mobile app that makes West Virginia legislation easier to follow, bringing bills, committees, legislators, and topics into one personal tracker.

Open live site Back to projects
RoleFull-stack developer
Product typeMobile app
FocusLegislative tracking and updates

Overview

Montani gives people a clearer way to follow West Virginia legislation. Instead of digging through scattered sources, users follow the bills, committees, legislators, and keywords they care about and see the actions and agendas connected to them in one place.

What I worked on

  • Built mobile flows to search and follow bills, people, committees, and keywords.
  • Connected legislative data sources and structured tracking around personal watchlists.
  • Designed clear, readable screens for following updates on the go.

Technology

iOSAndroidLegiScan dataSearch & watchlistsPush updates

Delivery

The app is live for iOS and Android, with a marketing site that explains the tracker and points people to the stores.
