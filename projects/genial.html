Case Study / AI Content Platform

Genial

An AI content platform that turns ideas sent by WhatsApp audio or text into ready-to-schedule posts for social media.

Open live app Learn more Back to projects
RoleFull-stack developer
Product typeAI content platform
FocusWhatsApp-driven content automation

Overview

Genial is a content-creation platform powered by AI agents. Instead of starting from a blank page, users send ideas, suggestions, or voice notes over WhatsApp, and the platform turns them into brainstorms, research, scripts, copy, images, and scheduled posts for social media.

What I worked on

  • Built the web experience for brainstorming ideas and managing a content calendar.
  • Connected WhatsApp so ideas sent as audio or text become ready-to-review content.
  • Integrated social accounts and scheduling for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Pinterest.

Technology

AI agentsWhatsApp APIBubble.ioInstagramFacebookLinkedInPinterest

Delivery

The platform is live, with WhatsApp as the primary input channel and a dashboard for reviewing, connecting accounts, and scheduling posts.
