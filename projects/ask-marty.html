Ask Marty

An AI companion app that helps older adults stay sharp, curious, and connected, with daily conversation, brain quizzes, gentle reminders, and a family dashboard.

Open live site App Store Google Play
RoleFull-stack developer
Product typeMobile app (iOS & Android)
FocusAI companion and family engagement

Overview

Ask Marty is a companion app built for older adults, with a warm, patient AI that chats, runs a short daily brain quiz, and delivers gentle reminders for medication and appointments. A separate family dashboard lets loved ones follow progress and send encouragement from anywhere.

What I worked on

  • Worked on the mobile app experience for iOS and Android.
  • Contributed to the companion chat, daily quiz, and reminder flows.
  • Supported the family dashboard, progress updates, and encouragement features.

Technology

Delivery

The product is live on the Apple App Store and Google Play, alongside a web presence for families and senior-living partners.
