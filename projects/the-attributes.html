The Attributes

An assessment platform combining Bubble.io product workflows, API integrations, and an interactive AI chatbot.

Overview

The Attributes is a live assessment product with complex workflows and integrations behind a simple user experience. The work required balancing feature delivery, maintenance, and dependable product behavior at scale.

What I worked on

  • Built and maintained Bubble.io workflows across the platform.
  • Connected external APIs and supported an interactive AI chatbot.
  • Worked on reliability and product improvements for an active user base.
