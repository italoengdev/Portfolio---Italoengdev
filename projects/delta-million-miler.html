Delta Million Miler

A web platform for Delta SkyMiles members to track Million Miler progress and status, with a secure code-based sign-in flow.

Open live product Back to projects
RoleFull-stack developer
Product typeWeb platform
FocusLoyalty status tracking

Overview

Delta Million Miler helps Delta SkyMiles members keep track of their Million Miler progress and status in one focused web experience. Members sign in with a secure, code-based flow and get a clear view of where they stand.

What I worked on

  • Built the web experience for tracking Million Miler progress and status.
  • Implemented a secure, code-based sign-in flow for SkyMiles members.
  • Structured account and progress data into a clear dashboard.
