WOMA CRM

A sales CRM built for Woox, a Mexican paint manufacturer, bringing customer context, sales activity, and operations into one system.

Open live product Client site Back to projects
RoleFull-stack developer
Product typeCRM / internal system
FocusSales and customer management

Overview

WOMA CRM is a sales CRM tailored to Woox, a Mexican paint manufacturer. It gives the commercial team one place to see customer context, track sales activity, and follow up without juggling spreadsheets and disconnected tools.

What I worked on

  • Built a sales CRM shaped around Woox's commercial operation.
  • Centralized customer context, sales activity, and follow-ups in one system.
  • Implemented authenticated access for the sales team.
