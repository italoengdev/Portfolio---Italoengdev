PetConnect MVP

A mobile-first marketplace connecting pet owners with services through booking, location, and messaging features.

Open live productBack to projects
RoleMobile product developer
Product typePet services marketplace
FocusMobile-first experience

Overview

PetConnect MVP was built around a simple mobile need: make it easier for pet owners to find and book services while keeping the experience useful on smaller screens.

What I worked on

  • Designed mobile-first flows with FlutterFlow.
  • Connected Firebase-backed data for the marketplace experience.
  • Implemented booking, location, and in-app messaging concepts.

Technology

FlutterFlowFirebaseMobile designLocation services

Delivery

The MVP demonstrates a focused path from service discovery to booking, with the flexibility to grow into a broader pet-services product.
