Bizaclia

A real estate portal that combines structured listings with AI agents, web scraping, and automated data workflows.

Open live productBack to projects
RoleAI-assisted full-stack developer

Overview

Bizaclia connects a real estate experience with automated routines that collect, transform, and surface useful property data. The project required product thinking across the user-facing portal and the systems behind it.

What I worked on

  • Designed Bubble.io workflows for the real estate experience.
  • Connected n8n routines to web-scraping and data-processing flows.
  • Applied AI-agent patterns to reduce manual operational work.

Delivery
